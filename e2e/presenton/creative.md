# Every Cup Starts on a Mountain

- 1,800 metres up, in volcanic soil
- Cool nights, slow growth, dense beans

# Cherry Red

- Coffee is a fruit before it is a drink
- Each cherry is picked by hand, at peak ripeness
- Two seeds inside. Those are the beans.

# The Long Dry

- Three weeks on raised beds in the sun
- Turned by hand, every hour
- The fruit shrinks, the sugars deepen

# First Crack

- 200°C in a spinning drum
- The beans pop like popcorn
- Thirty seconds too long and the fruit is gone

# A Thousand Aromas

> More aroma compounds than wine. Most of them are lost within a fortnight of roasting.

# Ninety Seconds

- Grind just before you brew
- Water just off the boil
- Wait. Then pour slowly.

# Your Turn

- Buy beans with a roast date on the bag
- Taste one cup black, slowly
- Ask where it grew
